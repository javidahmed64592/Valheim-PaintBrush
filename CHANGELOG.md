# Changelog

## Unreleased

- The brush has its own model, a flat paint brush whose bristle tip shows the colour you're painting with, instead of looking like a hammer.

## 0.1.0

First release.

- A paint brush, taken out and put away with `O`.
- Paint and Clear paint tools for pieces built by players, with a live preview on the piece you aim at.
- A colour picker in the brush menu: colour square, hue bar, R/G/B and strength sliders, hex field, palette and recent colours.
- Only players with the mod see the paint.
